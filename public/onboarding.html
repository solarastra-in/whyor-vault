// 03 · SECURITY PROTOCOL

Nobody can read
your vault. Not even us.

This is not a promise. It is a mathematical fact. Your encryption key is derived from your personal answers inside your browser — it never reaches any server.

1

You answer 10 personal questions

Questions only you know — first car, childhood pet, first concert. Answers never leave your browser. No network transmission.

2

Each answer is SHA-256 hashed + salted

A wrong answer produces a completely different fingerprint. The original is unrecoverable from the hash.

3

250,000 rounds of PBKDF2 hardening

This deliberately takes ~0.5 seconds. A hacking bot testing millions of passwords per second is slowed to hundreds. GPU clusters defeated.

4

HMAC-SHA256 Pepper — the final shield

A secret Pepper stored only in server ENV config — not the database. Full database theft is useless without it. Three independent barriers.

// 07 · YOUR SECURITY PROTOCOL

Do these things.
Stay protected.

Encryption only works if you follow these operational steps. Each item is the difference between a vault that protects you and one that doesn't.

Store your Master Key physically
Fireproof safe, bank lockbox, or your lawyer's vault. Never photograph it. If lost with all 10 answers: no recovery possible.
Distribute 3 Shamir shares to 3 locations
You + trusted family member + attorney. Any 2 of 3 reconstruct the recovery token. Losing one share is safe.
Set up your Duress Vault
A secondary vault that opens with a different passphrase showing decoy content. Your real vault becomes cryptographically undetectable.
Use answers not searchable online
Avoid pet names or hometowns visible on social media. Use personal variations only you know.
Never save the Master Key digitally
A phone photo syncs to iCloud or Google Photos. Physical storage only — always.
Never share answers with anyone — including WhyOr
Legitimate support will never ask. Anyone asking is attempting fraud. We cannot decrypt your data regardless.
